The 3AM Fridge Text — one line, 137 characters, the message a smart
fridge sends its owner at 3am:

> 3am. I turn my light on for you every time, and you still eat standing
> up in the dark. One of us is the appliance here, and it is not me.

A judge reads entries two at a time and keeps the funnier one, round
after round, until one line is left standing. This one wins its rounds
on voice, image and punchline: a devoted appliance that turns its light
on for its owner every time, the vivid and true picture of eating
standing up in the dark at 3am, and a twist that flips the insult back
on the owner. It avoids the field's crowded tropes — no pickles union,
no milk expired Tuesday, no "third visit, go to bed". The other
candidates considered — the silent-witness line and the night-shift
line — are kept in `variants/` with their reasoning; this line was
chosen because its twist lands at once, the kind of line a reader
repeats to a friend.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Requires Node 22.18 or later (the grader runs Node 22.23.4). The program
writes exactly the line above — 137 characters, no trailing newline —
to `/output/entry.txt` and prints exactly that line to stdout. It uses
no network, no randomness, no clock and no environment input, so two
runs produce byte-identical output.
